# ReTail App Store Preparation

## App Metadata

Name: ReTail

Category: Shopping / Lifestyle

Short description: Buy, sell, and donate pet supplies locally.

Long description: ReTail is a pet-focused marketplace that helps pet owners find affordable supplies nearby, sell items they no longer need, and donate useful supplies back into the community.

## Permissions

Camera: Used to add listing and message photos.

Photo Library: Used to upload listing and message photos.

Approximate Location: Used to show nearby listings and city/state context.

Push Notifications: Used for messages, favorites, reviews, listing status updates, and system notices.

## Privacy

Public marketplace screens show city/state and approximate distance only.

Exact home addresses, private email addresses, authentication identifiers, and precise coordinates are not displayed publicly.

## Release Readiness

Before TestFlight or Google Internal Testing, connect production Supabase, PostHog, Sentry, push credentials, App Store privacy answers, Google Play data safety answers, and final screenshots.
