# Marketing Email Compliance

ReTail stores an append-only marketing email preference but does not connect that preference to a marketing provider yet.

Before Crutchfield Interactive LLC begins sending commercial ReTail email, the selected provider and workflow must support:

- A working unsubscribe link in every commercial message.
- Suppression of opted-out and deleted-account addresses.
- Clear sender identification.
- Accurate From and Reply-To information.
- A valid business postal address in commercial messages.
- Prompt processing of unsubscribe requests.

Transactional, security, payment, account, notification, and essential service messages must remain separate from marketing eligibility.
