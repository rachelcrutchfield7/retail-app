# ReTail Community Guidelines

ReTail exists to help pet owners reuse supplies, save money, reduce waste, and support safer local exchanges.

## Be Honest

Use clear photos and accurate descriptions.

Include the real condition, size, brand, flaws, missing parts, pickup details, and whether the item is for sale, free, or donation.

Do not misrepresent an item, inflate condition, use unrelated photos, or list items that are not available.

## Pet Supplies Only

ReTail is for pet supplies.

Do not list live animals. This includes animals for sale, adoption, fostering, rehoming, breeding, stud services, trades, or giveaways.

Do not list prescription medications, recalled products, counterfeit goods, hazardous chemicals, stolen items, illegal items, adult content, hate speech, or unrelated services.

## Communicate Respectfully

Use messaging to ask questions and arrange pickup.

Do not harass, threaten, pressure, spam, send offensive content, or repeatedly contact someone who does not want to continue.

## Meet Safely

Meet in public places when possible.

Do not share exact home addresses publicly in listings.

Inspect items before completing a transaction and use your judgment before meeting someone in person.

## Support Rescues Responsibly

Rescues and volunteers may use ReTail to find or share supplies.

Urgent needs and donations must still follow the pet-supplies-only rule. Live animals may not be listed or transferred through ReTail.

## Report Problems

Report listings, profiles, or messages that involve:

- Live animals
- Spam
- Fraud
- Prohibited items
- Harassment
- Inappropriate content
- Duplicate listings
- Unsafe behavior

Reports help moderators keep ReTail trustworthy.
