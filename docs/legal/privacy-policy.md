# ReTail Privacy Policy

ReTail collects and uses information needed to operate a local pet-supply marketplace.

This document is a product policy draft and should be reviewed by legal counsel before public launch.

## Information ReTail Collects

ReTail may collect:

- Account information, such as email address and authentication status
- Public profile information, such as display name, username, avatar, bio, city, and state
- Listings, including titles, descriptions, photos, categories, condition, price, and general location
- Favorites, messages, reviews, reports, and notification records
- Approximate location information used for local marketplace discovery
- Device tokens for push notifications
- Analytics events, crash diagnostics, and basic device/app information

## Public Information

Listings, public profiles, review summaries, listing photos, city/state, approximate distance, and marketplace activity may be visible to other users.

ReTail does not publicly display exact home addresses, private email addresses, authentication identifiers, or street-level GPS coordinates.

## Messaging And Safety Records

Messages are intended for buyer and seller coordination.

Reports, moderation notes, account actions, and related records may be retained to investigate spam, fraud, harassment, prohibited listings, or unsafe behavior.

## Location Privacy

ReTail should show general location information such as city, state, and approximate distance.

Users should not include exact home addresses in public listing text or images.

## Account Deletion

Users can request account deletion.

When an account is deleted, profile information may be anonymized and active listings archived. Historical transaction, messaging, review, report, and moderation records may be retained when needed for safety, fraud prevention, audit history, or legal compliance.

## Notifications

ReTail may send notifications for messages, favorites, reviews, listing updates, account activity, safety alerts, or system announcements.

Users may manage notification preferences in Settings.

## Data Protection

ReTail should use secure authentication, encrypted connections, Row Level Security, access controls, and least-privilege practices to protect user data.

Private service credentials must never be exposed in the mobile app.
