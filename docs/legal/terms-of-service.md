# ReTail Terms of Service

ReTail is a local marketplace for buying, selling, and donating pet supplies. By using ReTail, users agree to keep the marketplace honest, respectful, and limited to permitted pet-supply items.

ReTail is owned and operated by Crutchfield Interactive LLC.

This document is a product policy draft and should be reviewed by legal counsel before public launch.

## Eligible Users

Users must be at least 18 years old to create or maintain a ReTail account.

Users must provide accurate account information and may not impersonate another person, organization, rescue, shelter, or business.

Users are responsible for keeping their login credentials secure and for activity that occurs through their account.

## Marketplace Use

Users may create listings for pet supplies they own or are authorized to sell or donate.

Listings must include accurate titles, descriptions, photos, condition details, price or free/donation status, and general location.

Users may not create misleading listings, duplicate spam listings, bait-and-switch listings, or listings for items they do not actually have available.

## Prohibited Listings

The following may not be listed, sold, donated, requested, or promoted on ReTail:

- Live animals
- Animal breeding, stud services, or animal rehoming listings
- Prescription medications
- Controlled substances
- Recalled pet products
- Counterfeit products
- Stolen goods
- Weapons
- Hazardous chemicals
- Illegal items
- Adult content
- Hate speech or discriminatory content
- Services unrelated to pet supplies

ReTail is for pet supplies only. It is not a platform for selling, adopting, fostering, trading, breeding, or transferring ownership of live animals.

## Local Exchanges

Users are responsible for arranging safe pickup, meetup, shipping, delivery, and payment details.

Seller email addresses, phone numbers, private street addresses, ZIP codes for regular marketplace users, and exact coordinates should not be publicly displayed by ReTail. Users may voluntarily share contact or pickup details through private messaging.

Users should meet in safe, public, well-lit locations when possible, inspect items before completing a transaction, keep communication in ReTail when possible, and follow applicable local laws.

## Protected Checkout, Shipping, Cancellations, And Refunds

When ReTail Protected Checkout is available and used, ReTail may maintain a transaction record and support path for order, payment, refund, cancellation, return, shipping, and payout issues.

Sellers should ship within 5 calendar days of purchase unless a shorter stated handling time applies. Before shipment, buyers may request cancellation. If the seller has not shipped within the allowed shipping window, the buyer may request cancellation or refund review.

After shipment, ordinary buyer's-remorse cancellation is not guaranteed. Changed mind, no longer needed, wrong size purchased by the buyer, or buyer preference change are not automatic refund reasons.

Refund or return support may be available for significant order problems, including materially not-as-described items, wrong item received, item damaged in transit, prohibited or dangerous items, fraudulent or counterfeit items where applicable, package never received, or another significant issue requiring admin review.

Buyers should report significant item-condition problems within 48 hours after confirmed delivery. Missing-package claims require support review and should not be automatically closed solely because a carrier says delivered.

Partial refunds may be handled by ReTail support/admin where appropriate. If the seller materially misrepresented the item, return shipping should generally be seller responsibility. Other cases may be determined by ReTail support case by case.

If users meet locally and choose to pay outside ReTail, ReTail payment/refund protection does not apply because ReTail did not process the payment. Users may still use ReTail messaging and safety/reporting tools.

## Messaging And Conduct

Users may use messaging to ask questions, coordinate pickup, and share relevant item photos.

Users may not harass, threaten, spam, scam, send inappropriate content, or pressure other users.

## Reports And Moderation

Users can report listings, users, and messages for spam, fraud, prohibited items, harassment, inappropriate content, or other safety concerns.

ReTail may remove listings, archive content, restrict accounts, suspend users, preserve moderation records, and investigate reports to protect the community.

Blocking another user should prevent new direct contact or messaging as designed. Blocking does not erase transaction records, reports, moderation history, or records admins may need to review.

## Rescue Donations

Verified rescues may receive physical-goods donations through ReTail, such as food, crates, carriers, bedding, litter, pet supplies, and enrichment items.

ReTail does not facilitate monetary donations to rescues at launch. Only verified rescue accounts may solicit physical-goods donations through rescue-specific donation or needs features.

## Account Deletion

Users may request account deletion. Profile information may be anonymized and active listings archived.

Historical transaction, message, report, and moderation records may be retained where needed for safety, fraud prevention, audit history, or legal compliance.

## No Guarantee

ReTail does not guarantee that listings are accurate, safe, available, legal, or free from defects.

Users are responsible for evaluating items and deciding whether to complete a transaction.
