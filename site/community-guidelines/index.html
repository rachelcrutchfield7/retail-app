Community Guidelines

Be Honest

Use clear photos and accurate descriptions. Include the real condition, size, brand, flaws, missing parts, pickup details, and whether the item is for sale, free, or donation.

Pet Supplies Only

Do not list live animals. This includes animals for sale, adoption, fostering, rehoming, breeding, stud services, trades, or giveaways.

Communicate Respectfully

Do not harass, threaten, pressure, spam, scam, or repeatedly contact someone who does not want to continue.

Meet Safely

Meet in public places when possible. Do not share exact home addresses publicly in listings.

Report Problems

Report listings, profiles, or messages that involve live animals, spam, fraud, prohibited items, harassment, inappropriate content, duplicate listings, or unsafe behavior.
