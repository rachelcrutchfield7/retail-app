ReTail

Privacy Policy

This policy is a product draft and should be reviewed by legal counsel before public launch.

Information We Collect

ReTail may collect account information, public profile details, listings, photos, favorites, messages, reviews, reports, notifications, approximate location information, analytics, diagnostics, and basic device/app information.

Public Information

Listings, profile details, listing photos, city/state, approximate distance, and marketplace activity may be visible to other users.

Location Privacy

ReTail should show general location information such as city, state, and approximate distance. It should not publicly display exact home addresses, private email addresses, authentication identifiers, or street-level GPS coordinates.

Safety Records

Reports, moderation notes, account actions, and related records may be retained to investigate spam, fraud, harassment, prohibited listings, or unsafe behavior.

Contact

General privacy questions can be sent to contact@retailpetapp.com. Account access or safety issues should be sent to support@retailpetapp.com.
